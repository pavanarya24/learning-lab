14 · Batch 2 · Free · $0/mo

Alpaca — Basic

IEX ONLY · ~2.5% OF VOLUME

Needs account + API keys (free). Real-time IEX feed via websocket, 30 symbols max; 200 API calls/min; 7+ years of history; REST snapshots 15 minutes delayed. Hard limit: IEX only — roughly 2.5% of US volume, so volume and intraday highs/lows differ from the full tape. Options data on free is "indicative" only, not real OPRA quotes (those are the $99/mo Plus tier).

Use — SPY / QQQ 1-min price bars for the entropy test (sketch 1) — price-based entropy is fine on IEX; do not use it for volume-delta work. For that, stay on Dukascopy ticks.

docs.alpaca.markets ↗

08 · Batch 1 · arXiv:2512.15720

Hidden Order in Trades Predicts the Size of Price Moves

2.89× LARGER MOVES

Singha, Dec 2025. Real-time order-flow entropy (15-state Markov, 1-second resolution) predicts the SIZE of intraday returns, not direction: entropy below the 5th percentile → subsequent 5-min absolute returns 2.89× larger (t = 12.41), directional accuracy 45% (chance). 38.5M SPY trades, 5-fold walk-forward validated.

09 · Batch 1 · arXiv:2511.06177

Push-response anomalies in high-frequency S&P 500 price series

SELL SHOCKS BOUNCE HARDER

Vlasiuk & Smirnov, Columbia, Nov 2025. Large negative intraday pushes are followed by stronger positive responses than equally large positive pushes — asymmetric liquidity replenishment after sell shocks. Tradable pockets + risk controls.

ALMGREN–CHRISS

18 · Batch 2 · arXiv:2610.34787

Do Round Numbers Hold Prices?

THEY DON'T HOLD

Jung & Lee, 26 Oct 2026. Prices cluster at round numbers, but on five Nasdaq stocks through 2025 the authors find round levels attract more liquidity yet do NOT hold price longer once depth is accounted for — departures are, if anything, biased away from round levels (Intel, SoFi).

arxiv.org ↗

19 · Batch 2 · arXiv:2610.33400

Tracking High-Frequency Trading Activity in China

PREDICTS 100MS–10MIN

Guo, Li, Qi & Sun, 24 Oct 2026 (v2 30 Oct). Defines "active exact-match orders" — limit orders priced exactly at the prevailing best opposite quote. They respond faster to quote updates, cancel faster, concentrate when depth imbalance is favourable and spreads are narrow, and predict mid-quote returns from 100 ms through 10 minutes. Caveat: Shenzhen data under T+1, no maker-taker — the mechanism may differ in US markets, so the proxy needs validating on US tape, not importing.

17 · Batch 2 · Free screener

Quantpedia — free Screener

Index, not a paper
1,000+ STRATEGIES

1,000+ academic strategies, 2,000+ source papers, 800+ out-of-sample backtests (QuantConnect code). Freemium — the honest split: the free strategies are the well-known systems; the lesser-known ones are Premium (paid, and the zero-spend rule says no). The free value is different: a paper-discovery index with plain-language rules, and its OOS backtests are a graveyard of ideas that died out-of-sample — e.g. the size factor's OOS backtest is slightly negative, pairs trading on country ETFs significantly negative.

Use — the ARE way: find a paper, read its rules, re-test it on your own symbols at double costs, worst fold kept. Never trust a paper's in-sample number.

23 · Batch 3 · arXiv:2610.14948

Exact Phase-Type Point Processes for Certified Measurement and Prediction of Event Streams

DIRECT HIT ~1S · ECHO 16–137S

Dutta, 10 Oct 2026. A phase-type Hawkes model with exact likelihoods and a closed-form shock response. Applied to six markets around 670 scheduled macro releases (2022–2026) with a placebo test: the direct reaction to CPI, payrolls, PPI and the FOMC statement is absorbed in about a second, while the market's own echoes stretch half of the response to 16–137 s. Best test likelihood on all five LOBSTER order-book streams.

arxiv.org ↗

24 · Batch 3 · arXiv:2610.26324

The Control Layer Behind Rule-Following Agents Trading Across Market Conditions

2,627 CLOSED POSITIONS

Naja, Odtallah, Van-Praag & May, submitted 19 Oct 2026 (v2 29 Oct). Tested whether four AI trading agents followed their strategies and risk rules: 2,627 closed positions, 12 Feb–27 Aug 2026, same asset universe and execution conditions. Relevance here is discipline, not a signal: it measures rule-breaking, which is exactly what ARE's pre-registration is built to prevent. Caveat: primary category is cs.AI (q-fin.TR secondary); results are trade-level, not account-level returns or proof of a lasting edge.

arxiv.org ↗

25 · Batch 4 · arXiv:2611.06607

Hard to Fool: A Bounded LLM Research Loop for Quantitative Finance, and What Two Dozen Campaigns Found

SHARPE ~3 → 0.26 ON HOLDOUT

Zhang, submitted 4 Nov 2026. An open-source agentic research loop built on the opposite premise to most LLM quant loops: the model proposes, and only trusted code decides — proposals are strict declarative JSON over a fixed vocabulary, so there is no model-written code to sandbox. Evaluation runs on immutable chronological stores split into discovery, validation and a final holdout that can be opened exactly once, after a durable receipt is written. Acceptance is a conjunction of absolute gates — positive edge and net P&L after costs, a positive weekly-cluster bootstrap lower bound, survival at elevated costs, per-year stability and sample floors — never a single score. Every campaign run to date is reported, across 0DTE options, overnight SPX verticals, daily ETF rules, technical-analysis premises, Kalshi crypto binaries, earnings options, off-exchange volume and a pre-registered alternative-data campaign; most hypotheses fail, and the failures are reported. The clearest proof the loop works is a failure: an LLM survivor that passed discovery and validation with Sharpe ratios near 3 fell to 0.26 on its single holdout use. Directly validates ARE's design (pre-registered hypotheses, walk-forward + Monte Carlo, worst fold kept) — and sharpens it: ARE's holdout should be opened exactly once, and acceptance should be a conjunction of gates, not one score. Caveat: the abstract says the loop is open-source but gives no repository URL on the abstract page — no repo is linked until one is verified from the paper itself.

arxiv.org ↗

26 · Batch 4 · arXiv:2611.05218

Beyond Anonymous Order Flow: Wallet Identity as a Feature for Short-Horizon Return Prediction

SPEARMAN ρ = 0.52 · 3.11 BPS

Ding & Zhai, submitted 3 Nov 2026. Reconstructs full-depth books from Hyperliquid's Level-4 record, which exposes persistent pseudonymous wallet addresses, and tests wallet identity as a feature for short-horizon return prediction. The 1–27 Jul 2026 record covers 17.1 billion messages, 14.3 million aggressive orders by 147,113 wallets, and $84.3 billion in taker notional across ten markets. Notional-weighted ten-second markout rankings for wallets persist across adjacent ten-day windows (Spearman ρ = 0.52); covariate-adjusted validation markouts reach 3.11 basis points in the top ventile. Eleven features describing previously scored top-decile wallets lift one-second out-of-sample R² from 10.88% — the abstract cuts off the second figure, so check the PDF before quoting it. Relevance here is tape-reading, not a signal to copy: the portable lesson is persistent-aggressor scoring — rank who is hitting the tape and whether the same aggressors keep winning. Caveat: crypto perpetuals only, on one venue; US equities have no wallet identity, so the feature itself does not port — only the persistence test does.

arxiv.org ↗

Strategies

7 sketches · all hypothesis only, not tested

Sketch 1 · From paper 8

Entropy-collapse ORB filter

Hypothesis — not tested
5TH %ILE → ~3× MOVE

Hypothesis: when 1-second order-flow entropy falls below its 5th percentile during the opening range, the next 5-minute move is ~3× larger than average.

1 →Compute entropy live from tape (order-flow platform already classifies trades Lee-Ready).
2 →Entropy below the 5th percentile? Take the ORB breakout, with wider targets.
3 →Entropy high? Skip the ORB trade — choppy, no edge.

ARE test — walk-forward on NQ (Dukascopy index CFD ticks) and SPY / QQQ, Monte Carlo reshuffle, worst fold kept.

Sketch 2 · From source 7

COT extremes as swing context

Hypothesis — not tested
|z| > 2

Hypothesis: Managed Money net-position z-score extremes flag stretched positioning.

1 →Weekly: Managed Money net position z-score (3-year window) for GC and NQ / ES.
2 →z > +2 or z < −2 and price diverges from positioning → flag a swing-reversal watch.
3 →Entries still come from ORB / VWAP / GEX levels — COT only sets the weekly bias.

ARE test — pre-register in ARE. COT is context, not timing (3-day lag).

Sketch 3 · From source 11

Own-GEX from MarketData.app

Hypothesis — not tested
3 REQ / MORNING · $0

Hypothesis: a gamma flip computed from the free morning chain predicts the day's magnet / pinning behaviour as well as THE MAP's levels do.

1 →Each morning before the open, pull QQQ, SPY and GLD chains (3 of the 100 daily requests).
2 →Nearest 0DTE / weekly expiry; gamma per strike from the quotes endpoint.
3 →Find the call wall (max call gamma), put wall (max put gamma) and zero-gamma flip.
4 →Trade only ORB / VWAP setups that agree with the flip side; log every day's walls vs THE MAP's and score which one called the high / low better.

First check — confirm the quotes endpoint returns Greeks on the free key before writing the puller. Cost: $0.

Sketch 4 · From paper 18

Round-number ORB test

Hypothesis — not tested
WITHIN 0.1% OF ROUND

Hypothesis: ORB breakouts through round-number levels do not follow through better than breakouts elsewhere, once depth is accounted for — so stop treating round GEX / ORB levels as magnets.

1 →Split past ORB trades: breakout level within 0.1% of a round number vs not.
2 →Compare follow-through (next 30-min move in breakout direction, at double costs) in ARE, worst fold kept.
3 →If confirmed: drop the round-number widening / tightening habit; size purely on the GEX flip + entropy state.

ARE test — pre-register in ARE before touching live data.

Sketch 5 · From paper 19

Exact-match flow as a tape filter

Hypothesis — not tested
70TH PERCENTILE

Hypothesis: a burst of active exact-match orders (limit orders repriced exactly onto the best opposite quote) during the opening range signals informed flow, and predicts 1–10 minute continuation in that direction.

1 →From the order-flow platform, count exact-match repricing events per minute in the opening range alongside depth imbalance.
2 →Take the ORB breakout only when exact-match share is above its own 70th percentile and imbalance agrees.
3 →Validate on US equities first — the paper is China data, and the mechanism may not transfer.

ARE test — pre-register in ARE; validate the proxy on US tape before trusting it.

Sketch 6 · From paper 23

News-echo ORB filter

Hypothesis — not tested
SKIP FIRST 3 MIN

Hypothesis: the first ~1 second after a scheduled release is the direct reaction; the next 16–137 s are the market echoing itself. ORB breakouts taken inside the echo window on release days are echo-chasing, not directional flow.

1 →On release days (CPI, payrolls, FOMC), skip ORB breakouts for the first 3 minutes after the release.
2 →Take only the breakout that survives past the echo window — or a VWAP reclaim after it.
3 →Compare follow-through vs the same ORB rule on non-release days, at double costs, worst fold kept.

ARE test — pre-register in ARE. If confirmed, the release-day ORB filter becomes a standing rule; if not, drop it.

Sketch 7 · From paper 25

ARE acceptance as a conjunction, holdout opened once

Hypothesis — not tested
ALL GATES · HOLDOUT ×1

Hypothesis: a strategy is accepted only if it passes all gates at once — positive net P&L after costs, a positive weekly-cluster bootstrap lower bound, survival at double costs, per-year stability, and a sample floor — and the final holdout is opened exactly once, after the receipt is written.

1 →Pre-register the full gate conjunction in ARE before running anything — no single-score acceptance.
2 →Run discovery + validation; a survivor gets exactly one holdout look, receipt written first.
3 →Fails the holdout? Frozen, not re-tuned — same rule as the frozen overlays.

ARE test — take one existing ARE candidate through the conjunction + single holdout, and record whether it survives or joins the frozen list. The Zhang paper's warning case is a Sharpe near 3 falling to 0.26 on holdout.
